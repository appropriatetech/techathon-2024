For @all who do have a team formed already, that's great! Make sure that you negotiate some structure among team. For example, you want to think about things like:

- _How will we communicate?_ I'm happy to create a separate Chat space for your team if you request. However, you could also use WhatsApp, Discord, or anything else. I suggest something more real-time than email, but email is a possibility as well.
- _How often will we meet, if at all?_ Will you have real-time meetings? I recommend at least one meeting to meet each other, learn how to pronounce each other's names, and get a sense of each other's personalities. But you might also want to have regular meetings to check in on progress.
- _How will we make decisions?_ You might want to think about how you'll make decisions as a team. Will you vote? Will you have a team leader who makes final decisions? Will you have a rotating leader? Will you make decisions by consensus?
- _How will we divide the work?_ This is really for after you decide on a project idea. Will you have a project manager who assigns tasks? Will you divide the work by skill set? Will you divide the work by interest?

Regarding the last point, I'm going to hold live, recorded sessions on project ideation this Tuesday and Thursday at 11am US Eastern Time (4pm UTC). I'll send an invitation to everyone that has a team formed, and share the recording afterwards.
